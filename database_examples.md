# World Database Query Examples

This document provides examples of natural language queries you can use to interact with the World database. The database contains information about:

## Available Data

1. Countries (table: country)
   - Basic info: name, code, continent, region
   - Demographics: population, life expectancy
   - Economics: GNP (Gross National Product)
   - Geography: surface area
   - Politics: government form, head of state
   - Capital city reference

2. Cities (table: city)
   - Name and ID
   - Country reference
   - District/State
   - Population

3. Languages (table: countrylanguage)
   - Language name
   - Country reference
   - Official status
   - Percentage of speakers

**Note**: This database only contains geographic, demographic, and language data. It does not contain information about companies, products, technology, or other business data.

## Query Examples

## Country Information

### Basic Queries
- "Tell me about Japan"
- "What is the population of India?"
- "Show me information about France"
- "Which country has the largest population?"
- "What is the capital of Brazil?"

### Geographic Queries
- "List all countries in South America"
- "Show me countries in the Mediterranean region"
- "Which countries are part of Scandinavia?"
- "What countries are in Southeast Asia?"
- "Tell me about island nations in the Pacific"
- "Which countries border the Indian Ocean?"

### Economic Analysis
- "What are the top 10 countries by GNP?"
- "Show me countries with highest GNP per capita"
- "List developing countries with rapid economic growth"
- "Which African countries have the highest GNP?"
- "Compare the economies of China and India"
- "Show me countries with GNP above 1 trillion"

### Population Statistics
- "Which countries have over 100 million people?"
- "List countries by population density"
- "What is the total population of Europe?"
- "Show me countries with declining population"
- "Which countries have the youngest population?"
- "Compare population between North and South America"

### Life Quality Metrics
- "Which countries have the highest life expectancy?"
- "Show me countries where people live longest"
- "List countries by healthcare quality"
- "What's the average life expectancy in Africa?"
- "Compare life expectancy between developed and developing nations"

## City Information

### General City Queries
- "List major cities in China"
- "What are the largest cities in Europe?"
- "Show me cities with population over 5 million"
- "Which city has the highest population density?"
- "Tell me about cities in the United States"

### Capital Cities
- "Show all capital cities in Asia"
- "Which capitals have over 1 million people?"
- "List European capital cities"
- "What are the newest capital cities?"
- "Compare populations of Asian capitals"

### Regional City Analysis
- "What are the major cities in California?"
- "Show me cities in the Tokyo metropolitan area"
- "List all cities in the Rhine-Ruhr region"
- "Which cities are in the Pearl River Delta?"
- "Tell me about cities in the Great Lakes region"

### City Demographics
- "Which Indian cities have over 5 million people?"
- "List fastest growing cities in China"
- "Show me the most densely populated cities"
- "What are the major port cities in Asia?"
- "Compare population between New York and Tokyo"

## Language Information

### Language Distribution
- "What languages are spoken in Switzerland?"
- "Show countries where English is official"
- "Which languages are most common in Africa?"
- "List all countries that speak Spanish"
- "Where is French an official language?"

### Language Statistics
- "What percentage of Belgium speaks Dutch?"
- "Show countries with multiple official languages"
- "Which languages are spoken by over 100 million people?"
- "List countries by English proficiency"
- "What are the most widely spoken languages?"

### Regional Language Patterns
- "What languages are spoken in the Caribbean?"
- "Show official languages of EU countries"
- "List indigenous languages of South America"
- "Which languages are common in Southeast Asia?"
- "Tell me about language diversity in India"

## Combined Queries

### Cross-Reference Queries
- "Show me capitals of Spanish-speaking countries"
- "List cities in countries with high life expectancy"
- "Which English-speaking countries have the highest GNP?"
- "Show populous cities in countries with low GNP"
- "Compare capitals of French-speaking African nations"

### Regional Analysis
- "What is the total GDP of English-speaking countries?"
- "Show me cities in countries with over 50 million people"
- "List multilingual countries with high life expectancy"
- "Which region has the most linguistic diversity?"
- "Compare cities in countries with similar GNP"

### Demographic Patterns
- "Show countries with many cities over 1 million people"
- "List regions where life expectancy correlates with GNP"
- "Which multilingual countries have the largest populations?"
- "Compare city sizes in rich vs poor countries"
- "Show relationship between language diversity and population"

## Tips for Querying

1. Be specific about what information you want
2. You can combine multiple criteria in one query
3. Feel free to ask for comparisons between different metrics
4. Use natural language - no need for database terminology
5. You can ask for data in different formats (lists, tables, etc.)
6. Feel free to ask follow-up questions for more details

## Note

The database contains information about:
- Countries (name, population, GNP, life expectancy, etc.)
- Cities (name, population, district)
- Languages (language name, percentage of speakers, official status)

You can combine these different types of information in your queries to get comprehensive insights about places, populations, and languages around the world.
